What’s inside

  • 133,137 structured observations across 14 sensor types
  • 741 original local events and 132,396 imported cloud honeynet observations
  • Chat, Alpaca, ShareGPT, prompt/completion, JSONL, and Parquet formats
  • Source-group-disjoint splits within each collection; cross-collection overlap is unknown
  • Local Ollama and generic Harness² evaluation bridges

Use responsibly

Original labels are weak deterministic thresholds. Imported rows have no fabricated answers. Neither establishes identity, intent, compromise, or botnet membership. Addresses, locations, credentials, commands, and raw payloads are excluded, but timing may still allow linkage with upstream records.
